## 2. Brand identity

- **Personality:** warm, resourceful, cheeky ("moches" worn as a badge of honour), generous, rooted.
- **Tone of voice:** first-person craft speech, zero corporate jargon; playful recasting of "ugly" as *delicious*.
- **Core values (from JOAFE purpose + interviews):** anti-waste, short supply chains, 100% péi sourcing, fair pay to producers (she *purchases* surplus — "pour qu'ils arrêtent de jeter" and stay motivated), professional insertion of vulnerable people, transmission (workshops).
- **Story:** Sandra arrives in Réunion (2015), is shocked by field-level waste, starts transforming seconds into preserves, founds the association (Aug 2022) in Saint-Louis, moves within weeks to Vavang'Art in L'Entre-Deux, upgrades in 2025 to a larger workshop with water access in the same site, crowdfunding to grow without subsidies yet.
- **Emotional appeal:** redemption of the imperfect → "Ce qui est moche dehors est merveilleux dedans."
- **Cultural identity:** deeply Réunionnais (péi produce: bringelle, pomélo, banane, mangue, goyave, letchi, combava; reuse of familiar jars/bocaux; participation in local markets & hotel tables like Le Dimitile).
- **Anti-waste positioning:** concrete and economic (buys surplus → transforms → sells → funds insertion), not greenwashed.
